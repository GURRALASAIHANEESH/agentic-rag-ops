LLaMA 2: Open Foundation and Fine-Tuned Chat Models — Overview

LLaMA 2 is a collection of pretrained and fine-tuned large language models
released by Meta AI in July 2023. It was made available for research and
commercial use. Model sizes range from 7B to 70B parameters.

Pretraining:
LLaMA 2 was trained on 2 trillion tokens from publicly available data sources.
The context length was extended from LLaMA 1's 2048 tokens to 4096 tokens.
Grouped-query attention (GQA) was introduced in the 34B and 70B models to
improve inference efficiency by reducing the memory footprint of key-value caches.

Fine-Tuning with RLHF:
LLaMA 2-Chat models were fine-tuned using Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) followed
by Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF). Two reward models were
trained: one for helpfulness and one for safety. The Ghost Attention (GAtt)
technique was introduced to improve multi-turn consistency.

Safety:
Meta conducted extensive red-teaming and safety evaluations. The models include
system prompts for safety and were evaluated on benchmarks including TruthfulQA,
ToxiGen, and BOLD.

Quantization for Local Deployment:
LLaMA 2 models can be quantized to 4-bit precision using GGUF format with
llama.cpp. A quantized 7B model (Q4_K_M) requires approximately 4GB of RAM
and can run on consumer hardware without a GPU. This makes it suitable for
local RAG deployments on developer laptops.

Performance:
On most benchmarks, LLaMA 2 70B is competitive with GPT-3.5. The 7B model
is the recommended default for local RAG systems due to its balance of quality
and resource consumption.
